The goal is to provide the most effective representation of Luxembourg’s external trade data.

- Who are Luxembourg’s main trading partners ? (exports and imports)? How have they evolved over time?
- What are the main categories of goods exported from and imported into Luxembourg ? How have these patterns changed over time?
- For a given product category, who are
Luxembourg’s principal trading partners
(exports and imports)?
- For a given partner country, what are the main
goods traded with Luxembourg? (using 2-digit
product codes) How can increasingly detailed levels
of information (up to 8-digit product codes) be
displayed while highlighting only the most
significant trade flows?
- Luxembourg’s trade compared with that of other countries?
- Do Luxembourg’s trade flows exhibit specific seasonal patterns, either overall or for particular product categories? How can their evolution best be visualized while filtering out purely seasonal effects?

Use the dataset from Eurostat on EU Trade, "[ds-045409] EU trade since 1988 by HS2-4-6 and CN8" (URL: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/ds-045409/legacyMultiFreq/table?category=ext_go.ext_go_detail)
